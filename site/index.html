Groovebox · Modular rack · Visuals

Four machines, one song, and a rack to patch them into.

Driftbox is a TR-808, a TR-909 and a pair of TB-303s, synthesised from scratch, with a modular rack beside them and visuals that play along. It started in the browser; this is the native app, for Mac, Windows, Android and Linux.

Download Try it in your browser

The groovebox: the song across the top, the pattern playing underneath, and the 303's knobs at the side, over the song's scene.

What's in it

The groovebox

Twenty-two drum voices across the 808 and the 909, and two 303s with accent and slide. Patterns of up to 64 steps, strung together into a song; flams, a pattern-controlled filter, swing per voice, and knob moves recorded as automation while it plays.

The rack

More than forty modules: oscillators, filters, envelopes, sequencers, samplers, effects and a Combinator whose four knobs play the whole patch. Turn the rack round to patch cables on the back. Host Audio Units on the Mac and VST 3 plug-ins on Windows.

Visuals that play along

Twenty-seven scenes, each listening to the drums and the bass as they play. Put them on a second screen or a projector, give the whole window to them, or play the filter with a finger on top.

Out into the world

Export the mix, a stem per voice, or a movie with its visuals; record a performance as you play it. On the Mac, the groovebox and the rack load as Audio Units in Logic and other hosts.

MIDI

Play the 303 and the drums from a keyboard, follow a MIDI clock or send one, and learn any rack knob to a controller.

Learn it as you go

A guide to the groovebox and the rack, a page for every module, and guided tours that watch you build a patch and tick each step as you do it.

On a phone, too

The same groovebox and rack on Android, laid out for fingers: pages of eight steps, a keyboard along the foot of the rack, and a pinch to zoom in on a module.

The songs are the same everywhere. A .driftbox file opens on every platform, and so does a song made in the browser.
